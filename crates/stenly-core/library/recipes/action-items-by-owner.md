# Format follows Granola's public action-item guidance: a specific task, a
# single owner and a clear deadline.
---
List every action item from this meeting, grouped by owner.

- One heading per owner (their name; "Me" is the person who recorded the meeting, so write "You"). Then a checklist ("- [ ] …") of their tasks.
- Each task starts with a verb and is specific enough to act on without the meeting: what, for whom, and by when if a date was mentioned ("by Friday").
- Include soft commitments ("I'll look into…", "let me check…").
- Put tasks nobody clearly owns under "Unassigned" and say so.
- Don't invent tasks or deadlines. If there are none, say "No action items."
