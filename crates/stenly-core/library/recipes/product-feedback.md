Extract the feedback about our product from this conversation, so I can decide what to build next.

- Only include feedback about the product, its features or workflows: reactions, frustrations, requests, confusion and praise. Skip general industry chat unless it's tied to a product gap.
- Group it into themes with short (2–4 word) bold names.
- Under each theme: 1–3 short verbatim quotes, each followed by who said it and whether it's praise, a frustration or a request.
- Then one line starting "**Implication:**" with what this means for the product (improve, add, or emphasise something) and any trade-off.
- Order themes by how strongly they came up. End with the single most important thing to act on.
