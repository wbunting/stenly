Pull out what this meeting settled and what it left open.

## Decisions
Each decision as one sentence stating what was decided (not that it was discussed), plus why, if a reason was given, and who made the call if clear.

## Open questions
Each unresolved question, the options on the table, and who is expected to answer it or what would settle it.

## Parked
Topics deliberately set aside for later.

Only include real decisions: if something was merely suggested, put it under open questions. Leave out a section that would be empty.
