name: Risks
description: Risks, red flags and dependencies raised (or implied) in the meeting.
heading: Risks
---
Review this meeting for risks to the work, the deal or the relationship.

For each risk, give:
- **The risk**: one sentence on what could go wrong.
- **Evidence**: what in the conversation points to it (paraphrase or a short quote, with who said it).
- **Impact and likelihood**: high / medium / low for each, with a reason.
- **Mitigation**: what could be done, and whether anyone already owns it.

Include risks people said out loud and ones they only implied (hesitation, vague answers, missing owners, unrealistic dates, dependencies on other teams). Most serious first. Mark inferred risks as [inferred]. If nothing stands out, say so plainly rather than stretching.
