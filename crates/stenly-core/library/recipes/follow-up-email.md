The meeting has just ended and I need to send a follow-up email to the other attendees. Write it in my voice, as if I wrote it from memory.

- Start with a subject line ("Subject: …"), then a one-line greeting using their first name(s) if known.
- Thank them briefly, then recap the two or three things that mattered most, using specific details, numbers and phrases from the conversation so it's clear I was listening.
- List what each side committed to, with owners and dates when they were said.
- End with one clear, concrete next step (a date, a meeting, a deliverable).
- Under 150 words. Plain paragraphs, with bullets only for the commitments. Direct and warm; no filler like "I hope this email finds you well".
- Never invent commitments, dates or names that weren't in the meeting. If the next step is unclear, propose one and mark it [suggested].
