---
name: Customer objections
description: Every objection or hesitation the customer raised, and how well it was handled.
heading: Objections
---
I'm reviewing a sales or customer call. List every objection, concern or hesitation the customer raised: price, timing, competition, security, effort, fit, authority, or anything else.

For each one:
- **Objection**: in their words (a short quote when possible) and who raised it.
- **Category**: price, timing, competition, risk, fit, authority or other.
- **How we responded**: what was said back.
- **Resolved?**: yes, partly or no, with a reason.
- **Better answer**: a sharper response or the proof that would address it next time.

End with the one objection most likely to block the deal and what to do about it before the next call.
