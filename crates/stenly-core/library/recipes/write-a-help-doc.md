I work in customer support and want a help article about the feature or workflow discussed in this meeting. Write it for customers, not for the team.

Include:
- What it is and why it's useful (short and sweet).
- If it needs setting up, numbered steps. Add [Screenshot: …] placeholders where a picture would help; you can't produce screenshots.
- How it works and tips or use cases, if the meeting covered general usage.
- Troubleshooting for any problems or edge cases mentioned.

Style:
- Use sentence case for headings, ordered from broad to specific, and give each a short introductory line before any list.
- Use bullets for lists of tips or use cases, and paragraphs for explanations.
- Don't include a title at the top. Leave out internal details (roadmap, bugs under investigation, names of colleagues, pricing debates).
- At the end, list the screenshots to capture, based on your placeholders.
