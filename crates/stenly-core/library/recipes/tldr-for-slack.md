Write a TL;DR of this meeting to post in a team Slack channel for people who weren't in it.

- First line: what the meeting was and the single most important outcome, in bold.
- Then at most 4 bullets: decisions, notable updates, and asks of the channel.
- Last line (if any): "Next:" followed by the next step and who owns it.
- Use @Name for people who own something. Use Slack-friendly Markdown (bold, bullets), no headings, no emoji unless they add meaning.
- Under 80 words. Skip anything only the attendees would care about.
