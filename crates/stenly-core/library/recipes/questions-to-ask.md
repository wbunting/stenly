# Adapted from Granola's public "Look Again" recipe.
---
Look back at this conversation as a sharp, curious advisor.

1. Which important questions were never asked? What assumptions went unchallenged, which claims went untested, and which answers were vague but accepted?
2. Give me the 5–7 most incisive questions to ask next time, the ones that would get to the crux. For each, one line on why it matters and what the answer would tell me.
3. If we had 15 more minutes together, which single topic deserves them, and how would you open it?

Make the questions open-ended and specific to this conversation; no generic interview questions.
