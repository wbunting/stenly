# Loosely inspired by Granola's public "Write PRD" and "Write a brief" recipes.
---
Turn this discussion into a one-page project brief that someone who wasn't there could act on.

## Problem
One or two sentences: who has the problem, what goes wrong, and why it matters now. Keep it solution-agnostic.

## Goal
What success looks like and how we'll measure it.

## Proposed approach
The solution or direction the group leaned towards, and alternatives considered and why they lost.

## Scope
In scope for the first version, and explicitly out of scope.

## Open questions and risks
## Next steps
Owners and dates where mentioned.

Keep it under a page. Mark anything you had to infer with [assumption], and anything missing that the team must decide with TODO:.
