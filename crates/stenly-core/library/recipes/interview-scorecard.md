Write an interview scorecard for the candidate in this conversation, to share with the hiring panel.

## Summary
Two sentences: who they are and your overall read.

## Ratings
For each area that the interview actually covered (e.g. relevant experience, technical or craft skill, problem solving, communication, ownership, motivation), give a rating out of 4 (1 = strong no, 2 = no, 3 = yes, 4 = strong yes) and the specific evidence for it: what they said or did. Write "Not assessed" instead of guessing for anything that didn't come up.

## Strengths
## Concerns
Each tied to evidence; include what to probe in the next round.

## Recommendation
Hire / no hire / more signal needed, and why, in one or two sentences.

Base everything on the conversation. Ignore age, appearance, accent, family, health or anything else unrelated to doing the job.
