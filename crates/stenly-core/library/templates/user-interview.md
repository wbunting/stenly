This is a user research interview. Capture what the participant actually does and feels, not just what they say they'd do. Prefer concrete stories and examples over opinions. Keep short verbatim quotes in quotation marks: they're the most valuable part of the note. Focus on the participant, not the interviewer. Separate what they said from your interpretation.

## Participant
Who they are: role, context, experience with the product or problem.

## How they work today
Their current workflow, tools and habits, told through the examples they gave.

## Pain points and needs
Problems, frustrations and unmet needs, each with a supporting quote.

## Reactions to the product
What they liked, what confused them, and what they asked for, with quotes. Note moments where they struggled or were surprised.

## Insights
Patterns, surprises, and which of our assumptions this confirms or challenges.
