This is a stand-up: a quick round where each person gives a status update. Keep it scannable. Make one section per person who gave an update, with their name (or speaker label) as the heading, and in it bullets for what they finished, what they're doing next, and anything blocking them (start blocker bullets with "Blocked:"). Put anything the group discussed or decided beyond the round in a final section.

## <Person's name>
Done, next, blockers. Short, concrete, with ticket or project names if said.

## Discussion
Anything discussed as a group after (or instead of) the round.
