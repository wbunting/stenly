This is a brainstorm. The goal is to keep every idea, not just the winners. Group ideas into themes (one section per theme, named after it) and give each idea one bullet with who suggested it when known, plus the strongest argument for or against it raised in the room. Don't judge ideas yourself.

## The challenge
The question or problem the session was trying to answer, and any constraints.

## <Theme>
One section per theme of ideas.

## Favourites
Ideas the group got most excited about or voted for, and why.

## Parked
Ideas set aside for later, and open questions to explore.
