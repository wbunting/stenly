This is a recurring team meeting with several agenda items. Make one section per agenda topic, named after the topic, in the order discussed. In each, capture the update or question, what was said about it (with who said it when that matters), and where it landed. Keep numbers, dates and names.

## Announcements
News and FYIs shared with the team (skip if there were none).

## <Agenda topic>
One section per topic: the update, the discussion and where it landed.

## Open questions
Things raised that weren't resolved, and who will follow up.
