This is a project kick-off. Write notes that will settle "what did we agree at the start?" arguments later: be exact about goals, scope, owners and dates.

## Goals
Why the project exists and how success will be measured (metrics, targets).

## Scope
What's in, what's explicitly out, and the deliverables.

## Roles
Who owns what, who decides, and who needs to be kept informed.

## Timeline
Milestones and dates, including the launch or delivery date.

## Risks and dependencies
What could delay or derail it, dependencies on other teams, and open questions.

## Ways of working
Check-in cadence, channels, tools and where documents live.
