This is a discovery call with a potential customer. The goal is to understand their situation and pain well enough to know whether and how we can help. Focus on what THEY said, not on our pitch. Use their own words for pains and quote short, telling phrases verbatim ("…"). Note who said what by name when known. Quantify wherever they gave numbers (hours, money, headcount, volume).

## About them
Company, team, their role, and the tools and process they use today.

## Pain points
The problems they described, in their words, and what each one costs them.

## What they've tried
Current workarounds, competitors or tools they use or have evaluated, and why those fall short.

## What success looks like
How they'd judge a solution, must-haves, and what "good" looks like in six months.

## Decision process
Who else is involved (champion, decision maker, blockers), budget, timeline and any deadline or event driving urgency.

## Fit and next steps
How well we seem to fit, open questions, and the next step both sides agreed to.
