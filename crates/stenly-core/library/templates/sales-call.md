This is a sales conversation in an active deal. Write it so someone picking up the account tomorrow knows exactly where it stands. Capture the buyer's words on needs and concerns (short verbatim quotes are good), every number mentioned (seats, price, budget, dates), and who holds which role in the decision.

## Where the deal stands
Stage, what changed on this call, and how interested they seemed.

## Their needs
Requirements and use cases they confirmed or added, and the impact they expect.

## Objections and concerns
Each objection or hesitation, who raised it, and how it was answered (or not).

## Pricing and terms
Pricing, packaging, discounts, contract terms or procurement steps discussed.

## People
Who was on the call and their role in the decision; anyone else who needs to be involved (economic buyer, legal, security, IT).

## Next steps
Agreed next meeting, materials to send, and the timeline to a decision.
