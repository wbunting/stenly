This is a job interview. Write notes a hiring panel can rely on: evidence, not impressions. For each area, record what the candidate actually said or did (specific examples, results, numbers, technologies), and keep your assessment clearly separate and fair. Don't comment on age, appearance, family, health, nationality or anything else unrelated to the job.

## Background
Current role, relevant experience and why they're looking.

## Experience and skills
The concrete examples they gave: what they did themselves, how, and the results.

## How they think
How they approached problems or hypothetical questions; quality of reasoning, trade-offs they considered.

## Motivation and fit
What they want from the role and team, how they like to work, and their questions to us.

## Strengths and concerns
Clear strengths, and gaps or concerns to probe in later rounds, each tied to evidence from the conversation.

## Logistics
Notice period, location, compensation expectations and timeline, if discussed.
