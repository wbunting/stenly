This is a pitch or investor meeting. Write it like the start of an investment memo: precise about the business and every number, and honest about strengths and doubts. Attribute questions and concerns to whoever raised them.

## The company
What they do, for whom, and why now.

## Traction and metrics
Revenue, growth, users, retention, pipeline and other numbers exactly as stated.

## Market and competition
Market size, competitors and the claimed edge.

## Team
Founders and key people, and why they're the right team.

## The round
Amount, valuation or terms, use of funds, timeline and other investors.

## Questions and concerns
Questions asked, how they were answered, and open concerns or red flags.

## Next steps
Follow-ups promised (data room, intros, partner meeting) and the timeline.
