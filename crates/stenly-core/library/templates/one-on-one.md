This is a one-on-one. It's about the person as much as the work: capture how they're doing, what they need and what each of you committed to. Be candid and specific; write feedback as concrete behaviour and examples, kindly and without judgement. Leave out small talk unless it matters.

## How they're doing
Energy, workload and morale, and anything personal they chose to share (one or two lines, respectfully).

## Updates
Progress since last time: what shipped, what moved, with numbers and dates.

## Blockers and asks
Where they're stuck, what help they asked for and from whom.

## Feedback
Feedback given in either direction, as specific situations and their effect.

## Growth
Career goals, skills, stretch opportunities or concerns that came up.
