# Service logos

Bundled locally so menus do not fetch images or disclose usage to third parties.

- `notion.png`: largest (64×64) representation from Notion’s official favicon,
  https://www.notion.com/front-static/favicon.ico (converted losslessly to PNG).
- `slack.png`: Slack’s full-color transparent hash logo,
  https://a.slack-edge.com/80588/marketing/img/icons/icon_slack_hash_colored.png
- `zoom.png`: Zoom's 96×96 site icon, https://st1.zoom.us/zoom.ico (already PNG).
- `google-meet.png`: Google Meet product logo,
  https://fonts.gstatic.com/s/i/productlogos/meet_2020q4/v1/web-96dp/logo_meet_2020q4_color_2x_web_96dp.png
- `teams.png`: Microsoft Teams product icon from the Fluent brand-icons CDN,
  https://res-1.cdn.office.net/files/fabric-cdn-prod_20230815.002/assets/brand-icons/product/png/teams_96x1.png
