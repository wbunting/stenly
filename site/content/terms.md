## Recording other people

Stenly records audio from your microphone and from the audio playing on your Mac. No bot joins the call, but you still need to tell people. **You are responsible for telling everyone you record, and for getting any consent that the laws where you and they are require.** Stenly does not post a recording notice and cannot check that anyone consented. If anyone objects, do not record. Do not use Stenly to record anyone in a way that is against the law or against someone's rights.

Please also be careful with what you share. A share link opens for anyone who has the full link, and a note you send to Notion, Slack or a webhook is subject to the rules of those services and of the people in the meeting.

## The share service

share.stenly.ai is a free, best-effort service. Notes are end-to-end encrypted, so we cannot read them or check what they contain. Do not use it to store or spread anything unlawful, and do not try to overload it. It limits how often you can upload. Shares expire (90 days by default) and we may remove a share, block abuse, or change or stop the service. Keep your own copy of anything you need. You can run your own share server from the source.
